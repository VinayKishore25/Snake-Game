🐍 Snake Game

📖 How to Play

🎮 Use Arrow Keys to control the snake direction
🍎 Eat red apples to grow longer and earn points
⚠️ Avoid hitting the walls or your own body
🏆 Score displayed in the top-left of the game
⬆ Move Up
⬇ Move Down
⬅ Move Left
➡ Move Right
💡 Pro Tips
• Plan your moves ahead to avoid trapping yourself
• The apple spawns randomly - stay alert!
• Each apple adds 1 point to your score
• Click "Restart Game" when game ends
Note: The score is shown in the game window (top-left corner). When the game ends, you'll see "GAME OVER" displayed. Refresh the page to play again.
